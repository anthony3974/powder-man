## New elements (v1.4)

### Earth

- [x] **Dirt** (powder): Water turns it into Mud. Seeds sprout on it.
- [x] **Mud** (powder): heavy and slow. Dries back into Dirt next to Fire or Lava. Lets off a little Methane.
- [x] **Clay** (solid): shape it, then Fire or Lava bakes it into Brick. Water slowly softens it into Mud.
- [x] **Brick** (solid): baked Clay. Resists Acid.
- [x] **Salt** (powder): melts Ice and Snow. Dissolves in Water into Salt Water.
- [x] **Salt Water** (liquid): heavier than Water and doesn't freeze. Boiling it leaves Salt behind. Rusts Metal faster.
- [x] **Ash** (powder): what burnt wood and plants leave. Light enough to float on Water.
- [x] **Obsidian** (solid): Lava touching Ice makes it. Acid can't touch it.

### Metals and heat

- [x] **Metal** (solid): Lava slowly melts it. Water slowly rusts it.
- [x] **Molten Metal** (liquid): hot, sets things alight, cools back into Metal.
- [x] **Rust** (powder): what wet Metal turns into.
- [x] **Coal** (powder): hard to light, then burns for a long time and leaves Ash.
- [x] **Thermite** (powder): burns white-hot and melts through Metal, Stone and Glass. Water doesn't stop it.
- [x] **Molten Glass** (liquid): Lava melts Glass into it. Sets back into Glass.

### Living things

- [x] **Seed** (powder): sprouts into a growing Plant on Mud (fast) or Dirt (slow).
- [x] **Vine** (solid): grows down from wherever you put it. Burns fast.
- [x] **Moss** (solid): creeps over Stone. Water slowly grows it on Stone.
- [x] **Fungus** (solid): spreads through Wood and Plant and eats them. Lets off Methane.
- [x] **Ant**: walks, climbs walls, digs tunnels in Dirt and Sand. Eats Plant and Seeds and breeds. Drowns in Water, burns.
- [x] **Worm**: wriggles through Dirt and Mud. Turns Ash into Dirt. Drowns slowly.

### Gases and weather

- [x] **Cloud** (gas): hangs at the top and rains. Gathers Steam.
- [x] **Lightning**: a bolt that zig-zags down, lights what it hits and turns Sand into Glass.
- [x] **Methane** (gas): rises and explodes when lit.
- [x] **Hydrogen** (gas): very light, goes pop. Acid on Metal makes it.
- [x] **Poison Gas** (gas): kills Plants, Seeds, Ants and Worms. Water soaks it up.
- [x] **Bubble** (gas): rises through liquids and pops at the surface.

### Odd and fun

- [x] **Nitro** (liquid): explodes when lit, or when it falls too far and lands hard.
- [x] **Sponge** (solid): soaks up Water. Heat dries it out as Steam.
- [x] **Bouncy Ball** (powder): bounces when it lands, a bit lower each time.
- [x] **Antimatter** (powder): wipes out whatever it touches, and itself, in a flash.
- [x] **Black Hole** (solid): pulls in everything nearby and deletes it.
- [x] **Portal In / Portal Out**: anything that touches an In portal comes out of an Out portal.

Also changed with this batch: burning wood and plants (Ember) now leave Ash or Smoke, and Acid fizzes Bubbles in Water.
