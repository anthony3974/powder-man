# Powder Man plan

Open ideas and notes. Completed and phone-tested tasks (through v1.7) have been removed; full history is in `git log -p plan.md`.

## Done, to test on the phone

- [x] Picker tabs: Basic, Earth, Heat, Boom, Life, Sky, Odd and All. Opens on the tab holding the slot's element, and a dot marks the tabs holding A's and B's.
- [x] Temperature: heat spreads between touching things (fast through Metal). Things melt, boil, dry, bake, set and catch fire at set temperatures, slowly, and hot things glow. **Heat view** button (H) colours the world by temperature. Check on the phone: speed with a full screen, the glow, and the new strip row fitting.
- [x] Electricity: Lightning hitting Metal, Molten Metal or Salt Water sends a white flash through everything connected. It lights things that burn, sets off explosives, fries Ants and Worms and warms the conductor.

## Players

- [ ] A stick man you control (the Powder Man from the app icon), dropped into the sandbox like an element.
  - Moves: walk left and right, jump, climb small steps, fall. On the phone, on-screen buttons (left, right, jump) that show while a player is in the world. On desktop, arrow keys or A/D and W/Space.
  - Lives in the world: stands on solids and powders, wades through liquids, gets pushed by explosions.
  - Can get hurt: burns in Fire and Lava, drowns, dissolves in Acid, is fried by electricity, and falls apart into Sand or Ash. Then it respawns, or you draw a new one.
  - Can use the pen: the player sprays the active slot's element from its hand, so you can build or fight with it.
  - Open questions: one player or two (two would need a second set of controls), and whether it's a normal picker element or its own button.
